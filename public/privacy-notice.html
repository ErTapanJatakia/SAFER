SAFER Account Privacy Notice

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Prototype notice, not legal advice or a compliance certification.

Demonstration data only. Personnel, incident, qualification, cybersecurity and checklist records remain in browser-local storage. They are not tenant-isolated or protected by account roles. Do not enter real personal, health, confidential, evidence or operational information.

Account information

When Firebase is configured, SAFER uses Firebase Authentication for email/password sign-in and email verification. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in SAFER settings documents.

SAFER stores an account profile in the Firestore users collection, keyed by Firebase UID. It contains UID, email, display name, role, status, organization identifier and timestamps. New accounts start with a pending role and pending status. A trusted project administrator must approve the account and assign a role.

Personal settings

Personal preferences are stored in a separate Firestore userSettings document keyed by the authenticated UID. This includes the selected existing theme, interface density, accessibility preferences, notification preferences, and language/date/time-zone settings.

Privacy requests

The Privacy & Data screen accepts access, correction and deletion requests for account profile and personal preferences. A submitted request is recorded for manual review. Submission does not mean the request has been completed. The current prototype does not automatically delete accounts or implement a full statutory grievance workflow.

Notifications

Notification switches store preferences only. SAFER does not currently deliver email alerts, SMS, browser push or background notifications.

Security boundaries

Firestore rules restrict account profile and personal-settings access to the signed-in UID. Users cannot use account settings to change their own role, account status or organization membership. Other Firestore data paths are denied pending a separately designed tenant-scoped records backend.

These controls do not protect browser-local operational demonstration data, and they do not establish a production-ready records-management system.

Retention and contact

Account data are retained in the Firebase project until handled through a separately approved administrative process. The deployment operator must publish and maintain an actual privacy/grievance contact address before inviting real users. This repository does not invent a contact address.
